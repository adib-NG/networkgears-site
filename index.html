What we build

Every layer of the stack, designed to work as one.

Pick a layer. Each one is something we design, build, and document, and each one is built to fit the layers around it.

hover or tap a layer
identity and access

One login, least privilege, no shared passwords.

We stand up a single identity provider, wire every app and device to it, and set access so people get exactly what their role needs. Offboarding becomes one click instead of a scavenger hunt.

  • SSO and MFA across every SaaS app, VPN, and workstation
  • Role-based groups that drive app access and device policy
  • Automated joiner, mover, and leaver workflows
OktaJumpCloudGoogle WorkspaceMicrosoft Entra
network design

Networks that assume nothing and still stay simple.

Flat networks are how one infected laptop becomes a company-wide incident. We design segmented, Zero Trust topologies with redundancy between sites, then document them so your team can change them with confidence.

  • VLAN and firewall design with a written policy for every rule
  • Site-to-site and remote access over mesh VPN, not port forwards
  • Dual-carrier internet with automatic failover

compute and storage

Hardware sized for where you will be in three years.

Servers, storage, racks, and power, specified and installed for high availability. We source hardware globally, build it, and leave you with a labeled rack and a diagram that matches it.

  • Server, storage, and GPU builds for AI and media workloads
  • Colocation and data center buildouts, end to end
  • Tested backups with a documented restore procedure

cloud and migration

Cloud where it earns its keep, nowhere else.

Not everything belongs in the cloud, and not everything belongs in a closet. We plan migrations workload by workload, with cost modeling up front and compliance requirements built into the architecture.

  • On-prem to AWS or Google Cloud migrations with cutover plans
  • Infrastructure as code so environments are reproducible
  • Monthly cost review so the bill never surprises you

devices and endpoints

A new hire opens the box and is working in twenty minutes.

Zero-touch enrollment means devices configure themselves from the moment they power on. Encryption, patching, and compliance are enforced by policy, not by reminders.

  • Mac, Windows, and mobile device management from one console
  • Disk encryption, OS updates, and app deployment by policy
  • Remote lock and wipe for lost or departing-employee devices

Security is how we build, not a line item we add.

Every layer above starts secure. On top of that we handle secrets, logging, and the evidence your auditors, insurers, and enterprise customers ask for.

  • Secrets management, encryption at rest and in transit
  • Centralized logging and alerting with a response runbook
  • SOC 2, HIPAA, and cyber-insurance questionnaire support
