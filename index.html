identity and access

One login, least privilege, no shared passwords.

network design

Networks that assume nothing and still stay simple.

Flat networks are how one infected laptop becomes a company-wide incident. We design segmented, Zero Trust topologies with redundancy between sites, then document them so your team can change them with confidence.

  • VLAN and firewall design with a written policy for every rule
  • Site-to-site and remote access over mesh VPN, not port forwards
  • Dual-carrier internet with automatic failover
